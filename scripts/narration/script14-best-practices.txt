OK so let me talk about job modes because this comes up a lot. There are four modes. Auto, Pause, Manual, and Hold.

Auto is where you want your jobs to be. The system handles reminders, sends notifications, moves things along. That's the default and it's the ideal.

Pause is for when you need to temporarily stop the automations. Like say you're waiting for a landlord to confirm something before you can move forward. You'd pause it, deal with whatever you need to deal with, and then put it back in Auto.

Manual means you're driving everything. No automations at all. Support jobs are always in Manual, and sometimes you'll put other jobs in Manual if the situation needs a more hands-on approach.

And then Hold. This is the one people misuse. Hold is only for when there's a genuine future date you're waiting for. Like the tenant's said they won't be back from holiday until the 15th and there's nothing you can do until then. That's a Hold.

What Hold is not for is parking jobs you haven't got round to yet. I've seen people stick jobs in Hold because their queue is busy and they want to deal with them later. Don't do that. If a job needs attention, keep it visible. Put it in Auto or Manual. Don't bury it in Hold and forget about it.
