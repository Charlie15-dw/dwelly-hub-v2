# Dwelly Training Portal — Staff Guide

Welcome to the Dwelly Training Portal. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to get started, work through your training, and make the most of the tools available to you.

Don't worry if it looks like a lot at first — the portal is designed to take you through things one step at a time, and this guide is here whenever you need it.

---

## What Is the Training Portal?

The Dwelly Training Portal is your central hub for everything related to your professional development at Dwelly. It brings together:

- Written training guides covering every area of the business
- Short video walkthroughs that show you exactly how tasks are done
- A knowledge base of deeper articles on specialist topics
- Quizzes and assessments to test what you've learned
- Your personal Competency Passport — a record of your verified skills and sign-off gates

Whether you're brand new to property management or joining with experience, the portal gives you a structured path to getting confident and competent in the Dwelly way of working.

---

## How to Log In

1. Open your web browser and go to the Dwelly Training Portal URL (your manager will give you this link on your first day).
2. Enter your work email address and the temporary password you were sent.
3. You'll be prompted to set a new password on first login — choose something you'll remember.
4. Once logged in, you'll land on your **Dashboard**. This is your home base.

> **Can't log in?** Check your spam folder for the welcome email. If you're still stuck, speak to your manager or contact the support email listed at the bottom of the login page.

---

## Your Dashboard

The Dashboard is the first thing you'll see when you log in. It gives you a live snapshot of where you are in your training:

- **Your Journey** — a visual path showing where you are across the six stages of training (Learning Profile → Training Guide → Video Modules → Knowledge Base → Assessments → Competency Passport)
- **Overall Progress** — a percentage showing how much of your training programme is complete
- **Modules Completed** — a count of assessments you've passed out of 15 total
- **Avg Assessment Score** — your average score across all assessments attempted
- **Competencies Signed Off** — how many competency sign-off gates have been formally approved by your manager
- **Modules Up Next** — the next video modules waiting for you

Spend a moment getting familiar with the Dashboard — you'll be coming back to it regularly.

---

## The Recommended Learning Journey

The portal is built around a recommended order. Follow this sequence and you'll build your knowledge in a logical, layered way:

1. Learning Profile
2. Training Guide
3. Video Modules
4. Knowledge Base
5. Assessments
6. Competency Passport

Each section is explained in detail below.

---

## Step 1: Learning Profile

**Do this first — it only takes about 5 minutes.**

The Learning Profile is a short set of questions about how you prefer to take in new information — for example, do you like reading first or watching first? Do you prefer working through things in one go, or in short bursts?

Your answers help your manager understand how best to support you, and a summary is visible to them in the Manager Dashboard.

### How to complete it

1. From the Dashboard or the main navigation, click **Learning Profile**.
2. Read each question and select the answer that best describes you — there are no right or wrong answers.
3. Click **Submit** when you're done.

**Important:** The Learning Profile can only be completed once. Once submitted, you will see a summary of your answers and your manager's guidance notes — there is no option to retake it. Take your time and answer honestly.

---

## Step 2: Training Guide

**Read these before anything else.**

The Training Guide section contains written guides covering every major area of Dwelly's operations. Think of these as your foundational reference material.

### Sections covered

- **General Navigation** — how to get around the Dwelly platform
- **Maintenance** — how to manage repair requests, contractor relationships, and property upkeep
- **Compliance** — legal obligations, certificates, licensing requirements, and key deadlines
- **Move-Outs** — the end-of-tenancy process from notice to deposit return
- **Inspections** — how to conduct, record, and follow up on property inspections
- **Right to Rent** — how to verify tenant eligibility during a tenancy, including share code verification and visual likeness checks
- **Support** — tenant communication, handling complaints, and escalation processes
- **Best Practices** and **Auto vs Hold** guidance

### How to use the Training Guide

1. From the main navigation, click **Training Guide**.
2. Select a section (start with whichever area is most relevant to your role, or work through them in order).
3. Read through the guide carefully. You can use headings to jump to specific topics within each section.
4. Use the **search bar** at the top of the screen to find anything specific across the whole portal.

> **Tip:** The Training Guides are also useful as ongoing reference material after your initial training. Come back to them whenever you need a reminder of how a process works.

---

## Step 3: Video Modules

**Watch these after reading the Training Guide for each section.**

The Video Modules section contains short, practical video walkthroughs. Each topic area has two videos:

- **Part 1 of 2 — Creating**: how to create or initiate something (for example, raising a maintenance job or logging an inspection).
- **Part 2 of 2 — Actioning**: how to action, update, or close out a task once it's in progress.

The videos are concise and to the point — typically a few minutes each.

### Sections with video modules

All five core areas have a 1/2 and 2/2 module: Maintenance, Compliance, Inspections, Move-Outs, and Support — 10 video modules in total. None of the modules are locked, so you can work through them in whichever order suits you.

### How to watch a video module

1. From the main navigation, click **Video Modules**.
2. Choose a section that matches what you've read in the Training Guide.
3. Always watch Part 1 before Part 2.

> **Tip:** If you're a visual learner, you may want to watch the video for a section before reading the written guide — that's completely fine. Use whichever order works for you.

---

## Step 4: Knowledge Base

**Deepen your understanding of specialist topics.**

The Knowledge Base contains longer, more detailed articles on specific subjects that require extra context. These aren't things you need to memorise, but they're important to understand — especially if they're relevant to your role.

### Topics covered include

- Tenancy law essentials
- Handling disrepair claims
- Difficult tenant conversations
- Understanding a P&L
- HMO licensing
- Deposit schemes
- Damp and mould
- And more

### How to use the Knowledge Base

1. From the main navigation, click **Knowledge Base**.
2. Browse by topic, or use the search bar to find a specific subject.
3. Read articles relevant to your role — your manager may direct you to particular ones during your induction.

> **Tip:** You don't need to read every article before moving on to assessments. Focus on the ones most relevant to your current role.

---

## Step 5: Assessments

**This is where you prove what you know.**

Assessments are an important part of your training. They're not there to catch you out — they're designed to help you (and your manager) confirm that you've understood what you've learned and are ready to apply it.

### Types of assessment

There are **15 assessments in total** across the programme:

1. **10 Video Module Assessments** — one per video module (2 per section across Maintenance, Compliance, Inspections, Move-Outs, and Support)
2. **3 Knowledge Base Assessments** — covering Difficult Conversations, Handling Disrepair Claims, and Understanding a P&L
3. **Full Programme Assessment** — covers all five video module sections combined
4. **Master Assessment** — the final assessment; scenario-based, covering everything across the whole programme

### Pass mark and what happens if you don't pass

- The pass mark for all assessments is **80%**.
- If you score below 80%, you'll be shown which areas to review. You can retake the assessment after going back over the relevant material.
- There is no penalty for retaking.
- If you're struggling with a particular topic after two attempts, speak to your manager before trying again.

### The Master Assessment

The Master Assessment is the final milestone of your initial training. Attempt it only once you've completed all other assessments.

- It is scenario-based — it tests applied judgement, not just recall.
- The same 80% pass mark applies.
- Passing the Master Assessment is a key milestone and formally marks the end of your induction training.

### How to take an assessment

1. From the main navigation, click **Assessments**.
2. Choose the assessment you're ready to take.
3. Read each question carefully before selecting your answer.
4. Submit when you've answered all questions.
5. Your result will be shown immediately, along with feedback on any incorrect answers.

> **Note:** Your manager can see all of your answers — including correct ones — so they can understand your knowledge in full, not just where you went wrong.

---

## Step 6: Competency Passport

**Your personal record of verified skills.**

Your Competency Passport is a record of the competencies you've been signed off on, your assessment progress, and key milestones like T&C supervision and annual re-certification.

### What it contains

- **Competency Sign-Off Gates** — specific high-risk areas that require your manager's formal sign-off before you can work independently
- **Assessment progress** — a live count of assessments passed out of 15
- **T&C Supervision tracker** — your 90-day supervised period milestones
- **Annual Re-Certification** — a reminder of when your compliance re-cert is due
- **Achievement Badges** — earned as you reach milestones throughout the programme

### What sign-offs mean

A sign-off is your manager's formal confirmation that you've demonstrated a particular skill to the required standard — not just in theory, but in practice.

Sign-offs happen over time as you build experience on the job. They're not something to stress about in your first week — they come naturally as you work through tasks with your manager.

### Viewing your Competency Passport

1. From the main navigation, click **Competency Passport**.
2. You'll see your sign-off gates, current assessment progress, supervision tracker, and achievement badges.

> **Tip:** Your Competency Passport can be used to demonstrate your development during appraisals or reviews.

## Reference Sections

These sections are available at any time and are worth bookmarking for quick access.

### Glossary

The Glossary contains plain-English definitions of property management terms you'll come across in your work — things like "AST", "Right to Rent", "share code", "Section 8", "EPC", "HMO", and dozens more.

If you come across a term you don't recognise while reading the Training Guides or Knowledge Base, check the Glossary first.

**How to use it:** Click **Glossary** in the navigation. Use the search bar or browse alphabetically.

---

### Emergency Procedures

This section contains quick-reference guidance for urgent situations. It covers:

- Fire
- Gas leak or suspected gas leak
- Flooding
- Boiler failure
- Other property emergencies

**How to use it:** Click **Emergency Procedures** in the navigation. Each scenario has a short, clearly numbered list of steps to follow.

> **Important:** Familiarise yourself with this section in your first week. You may need to act quickly in a real situation.

## For Property Managers: Recap & Refresh

If you're in a Property Manager role, you'll have access to an additional section called **Recap & Refresh**.

This is a monthly knowledge check designed to keep your understanding sharp over time. It draws from all areas of the portal and presents you with a short set of questions each month.

- The quizzes are short — usually 10 to 15 questions.
- They're not high-stakes — they're designed for refreshing and reinforcing, not testing.
- Your results are visible to your manager on the Manager Dashboard.

You'll receive a notification each month when your Recap & Refresh quiz is ready. It takes about 10 minutes to complete.

## The Portal Search Bar

The search bar at the top of every page lets you search across the entire portal — video modules, knowledge base articles, and glossary terms — all in one place. If you're looking for something specific, type a keyword and it will pull up anything relevant.

## Tips for Getting the Most Out of the Portal

- **Follow the recommended order.** Each section builds on the last. Starting with the Learning Profile and working through in sequence gives you the best foundation.
- **Don't rush assessments.** Go back and re-read a section if you're not feeling confident before attempting the quiz.
- **Use the search bar.** If you need to find something specific, search before scrolling.
- **Keep your Dashboard in view.** Checking in at the start of each week shows you exactly what's next.
- **Ask your manager.** The portal is a tool to support your training, not replace the people around you. If something doesn't make sense or you're unsure how a process applies in practice, ask.

## If Something Goes Wrong or You Get Stuck

### Technical issues

- **Page not loading or error message:** Try refreshing the page. If that doesn't help, log out and log back in.
- **Video won't play:** Check your internet connection. If you're on a VPN, try disconnecting and reconnecting.
- **Assessment not saving your result:** Don't retake it immediately — speak to your manager first, as your result may have been recorded even if it didn't display correctly.
- **Can't log in:** Check your email for a reset link. If you didn't receive one, ask your manager to request a reset on your behalf.

### Content questions

- If you read something in the portal that doesn't match what you've been told by a colleague or manager, raise it — the portal is kept up to date but your manager is always the best source of truth for how things work at your specific office.

### Assessments

- If you fail an assessment twice and are still unsure about the material, speak to your manager before attempting it again. They may want to walk through the topic with you in person.

### General support

If you have any other issues with the portal — whether technical or content-related — contact your line manager in the first instance. They can escalate to the portal admin team if needed.

---

## A Final Note

Learning a new role takes time, and the portal is designed to support that process — not rush it. Work through things at a pace that allows things to sink in, ask questions when you're unsure, and remember that everyone who's completed this training started exactly where you are now.

Good luck, and welcome to the team.
